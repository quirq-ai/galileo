galileo

Launcher

Every app on this machine, at its own address.

galileo is a local gateway. It puts every folder of your XO root, and any app you add, behind this one port, each at name.localhost:4100, and carries the XO toolbar into their pages.

How it works

Addresses

  • acme.localhost:4100 opens an app's default version.
  • dev.acme.localhost:4100 opens one version of it.
  • acme.localhost:4100/dev/… sends you to that version.
  • Each address is its own origin, so apps never share cookies, storage or service workers.

Where apps come from

  • Every folder in the XO root is an app named after it: Gamma Notes becomes gamma-notes.
  • An xo-project's .xo/project.json gives it its display name.
  • Add anything else, a port or a deployment, in the launcher.
  • Folders come and go live. Hidden folders and node_modules never become apps.

Running them

  • A folder with a dev command (its .claude/launch.json, or dev or start in package.json) gets its dev server.
  • That server starts when you first open the app and stops after 30 idle minutes.
  • One already running from the folder is used as it is.
  • Any other folder is served as files: its index.html, or a listing.

The toolbar

  • Every HTML page gets the XO toolbar: inspect, comment, capture, record, ask the agent.
  • Comments, captures and designs stay in galileo's data/ folder.
  • Each app's toolbar design is set in the launcher.

For programs

  • GET /__xo/health: the app names and roots, cheap enough to poll.
  • GET /__xo/api/targets: every app in detail, for pages on this origin.
  • Mounted in another server, as xo-client does, galileo answers only *.localhost and /__xo/*.
