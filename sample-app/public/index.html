What it does for you

Backlinks without effort

Mention a project and the note shows up on that project's page, with the sentence that mentioned it.

Decisions you can find

Mark a line as a decision and it lands in one list per project, with who decided and when.

Plain text, always

Every note is a Markdown file you can export at any time. Nothing is locked in a database.

Questions

Can I import my existing notes?

Yes. Drop a folder of Markdown files or a Notion export and Acme Notes links them on import.

Does it work offline?

Notes are stored on your device first and sync when you are back online.

Who can see my notes?

Only the people you share a space with. Links are private unless you publish them.
