About Acme Notes

We are four people who kept losing decisions in meeting notes.

Acme Notes started as a script that searched our notes folder for the word "decided". It grew into a notes app that treats links, people and decisions as first-class things, while keeping every note a plain Markdown file.

Your notes should outlive the app you wrote them in.

Acme Notes is a sample app for the XO toolbar demo.
